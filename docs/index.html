About.

I'm a Penn State graduate moving into AI and data center operations, with a background in IT support and game design.

I earned my BA in Digital Media, Art and Technology in May 2025, with a minor in Game Development, after studying computer networking at Erie County Technical School. I'm based in Erie, Pennsylvania.

My hands-on experience is in IT. I spent nearly three years as a student technician fixing hardware and answering help desk tickets for campus staff and faculty, and as an IT intern with the Erie County Convention Center Authority I installed IP cameras across the Bayfront Convention Center and the hotel next door.

I've completed Google's AI certificates and I build with Google AI Studio and Claude Code, including my own journaling app. In January I'm starting Penn State's online Master's in Artificial Intelligence Engineering. My creative work in game environments, animation, and design is where I learned to build things people actually use.
